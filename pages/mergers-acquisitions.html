Institutional Discipline

The 4-Phase M&A Execution Blueprint

Transactions fail when diligence is rushed or integration planning begins after closing. Stackly enforces a synchronized diligence-to-value framework.

1

Mandate Scoping & Synergies Valuation

Financial modeling, baseline EBITDA normalization, and confidential market sounding across pre-cleared institutional buyers or targets.

2

Confidential Virtual Data Room & Bid Auction

Bespoke information memorandums, non-binding LOI auctions, and management presentation staging ensuring valuation tension.

3

Definitive Documentation & Regulatory Filing

Purchase and Sale Agreement (PSA) covenants, escrow arrangements, reps & warranties insurance (RWI), and antitrust submissions.

4

Day-1 Closing & 100-Day Synergy Harvest

Treasury transfer execution, stakeholder communication protocols, and executive synergy tracking dashboard hand-off.
