Frequently Asked Inquiries

Key guidelines regarding initial partner consultations, mandate qualification, and retainer terms.

What is the typical timeline for preliminary transaction assessment?

Upon submission of our bilateral NDA and initial financial parameters, Stackly delivers an executive memorandum within 3 to 5 business days detailing valuation range, buyer appetite, and transaction feasibility.

Does Stackly represent both buy-side and sell-side mandates?

Yes. We represent private equity funds, strategic corporate acquirers, and founder-owned businesses. To eliminate conflict of interest, our compliance committee conducts exhaustive counterparty clearance prior to formal engagement.

How are client confidential materials stored and handled?

All corporate artifacts, financial balance sheets, and cap tables are hosted inside SOC 2 Type II compliant virtual data rooms with granular watermarking and time-limited access keys.
