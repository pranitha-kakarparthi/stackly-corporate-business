Institutional Security

Client Escrow & Fiduciary Safeguards

How Stackly safeguards client retainers, confidentiality, and transaction intelligence with zero institutional exposure.

Segregated Custodial Escrow

Client retainers and milestone disbursements are held exclusively in segregated, third-party FDIC/FINMA-insured institutional accounts at Tier-1 depository banks until verified deliverables are executed.

Milestone-Gated Deliverables

Every retainer phase is tied to auditable milestones—from confidential information memorandums (CIM) and financial models to fairness opinions and regulatory submissions authenticated by client counsel.

Air-Gapped Confidentiality

Deal room documents and valuation metrics are protected by zero-knowledge 256-bit AES encryption with localized data residency routing, strict non-circumvention charters, and zero conflicting positions.
