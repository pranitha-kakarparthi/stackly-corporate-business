Methodical Execution

The Crisis-to-Growth Phased Playbook

Turnarounds require strict chronological discipline. Our structured 4-phase framework converts crisis into enduring operational excellence.

1

Days 1–14: Liquidity Control & Cash Conservation

Immediate cash receipts sweep, emergency procurement moratoria, weekly rolling 13-week model, and establishment of restructuring war room.

2

Days 15–45: Diagnostic Audit & Stakeholder Consensus

Granular operational autopsy, lender transparency reports, creditor forbearance negotiations, and identification of quick EBITDA wins.

3

Days 46–90: Core Restructuring & Margin Expansion

Executing workforce rightsizing, vendor renegotiation, unprofitable contract terminations, and production bottleneck resolution.

4

Day 90+: Sustained Growth & Exit Readiness

Recapitalization or refinancing execution, governance transition back to corporate officers, and long-term enterprise value tracking.
